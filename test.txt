Source: zhang_2022_third_generation_thermodynamic_descriptions_for_ta_cr_and_ta.html

This study developed third-generation thermodynamic descriptions for the Ta-Cr and Ta-V binary systems, with a particular focus on the C14 and C15 Laves phases crucial for refractory high-entropy alloys (RHEAs). Using the CALPHAD approach, the authors established Gibbs energy expressions for pure Cr and V, and evaluated thermodynamic parameters for the Laves phases by combining theoretical computations, experimental data, and semiempirical relations. The research aimed to accurately describe thermodynamic properties, thermal vacancy contributions, and phase equilibria across the entire composition and temperature ranges. The calculated phase diagrams and thermodynamic properties demonstrated excellent agreement with experimental data, even down to 0 K, thereby validating the reliability and robustness of the developed thermodynamic models. This work contributes to building reliable thermodynamic databases for designing Laves-phase-containing RHEAs.
